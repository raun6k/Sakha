# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-selected React/Vite, React Router declarative routes, Query, Zustand, Tailwind, Radix and Lucide. Shared strict TypeScript/Zod contracts.
## Users
Farmers reviewing recorded farm context, evidence, farming options and decisions in a local browser.
## Product Purpose
One farming Command Center with A0 Chief of Staff and eight specialist workspaces. Useful evidence and clear decisions without inventing measurements.
## Operating Context
One local loopback backend; no farmer login. Monitoring pauses with backend shutdown or laptop sleep. Runtime credentials are operator configuration.
## Capabilities and Constraints
All P00–P19, U01–U09 and J01–J09 planned in canonical local docs. Proposal, farmer approval and actual work confirmation are distinct. Missing source/model capabilities remain visible; scenario inputs never change actual facts.
## Brand Commitments
User-supplied evergreen sidebar, ivory canvas, serif headings, sans controls, green actions, gold accents, white cards and farm panorama. Original references remain unchanged.
## Evidence on Hand
Ten reference screenshots guide presentation only. Backend capabilities govern source and model success. Decorative panorama is generated, never evidence of the farmer's real land.
## Product Principles
Persist before claiming success. One owner for facts. Preserve evidence/context. Show uncertainty and limits. Keep private collection optional and purpose-scoped.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard/focus/touch, readable contrast, responsive mobile bottom navigation, reduced motion and chart text alternatives. English is the implemented language; no unsupported language claims.
