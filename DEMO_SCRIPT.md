# Demo script

About two and a half minutes. Record the phone-width app (390px is enough) and the How-your-team-works sheet. Start from a seeded database: `pnpm seed:demo && pnpm dev`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5173.

## 1. Problem hook — 0:00

**On screen:** Home. Let the drought-coloured sky and the risk gauge settle on **Act now**.

**Voiceover:** Most Indian farms are under two hectares, and an El Niño year is when a dry week hurts. Kavita's forecast is about 1 mm of rain, and the saved NOAA index is El Niño at plus 2.16.

## 2. Kavita — 0:18

**On screen:** Open **My Farm**. Show Kalmeshwar, soybean, pods drying, cotton, borewell.

**Voiceover:** This is Kavita Meshram. Soybean on 1.6 hectares, pods drying, cotton on 0.8, and one borewell she cannot waste.

## 3. Daily Brief — 0:36

**On screen:** Back to **Home**. Swipe the Daily Brief so the next card peeks, and tap a dot. Pause on Megha's rain and El Niño line, then Paisa's rupee line.

**Voiceover:** The Daily Brief is her team. Megha has the dry week and El Niño. Paisa has the Nagpur mandi.

## 4. Team chat — 0:54

**On screen:** Open **Team**, then **Megha**. Scroll the saved rain answer. Point at the blue bubble and "Checked by Sakha".

**Voiceover:** She can talk to Megha in Hindi or English. The rain number is Open-Meteo, and Sakha has already checked it.

## 5. Cedar block — 1:12

**On screen:** In Megha's chat, scroll to "What is today's mandi price?". Hold on the amber chip.

**Voiceover:** Megha was asked for the mandi price. Cedar blocks that. The chip says Megha can't do this, Paisa can, and the line under it is the real Cedar denial.

## 6. The plan, in rupees — 1:32

**On screen:** Open **My Plan**. Show the cut-and-sell tasks, cost ₹5,600, benefit ₹12,960.

**Voiceover:** The plan is to cut this dry week and aim for six thousand one hundred seventy-five rupees a quintal, against an MSP of five thousand seven hundred eight. Harvest labour is five thousand six hundred. Twelve thousand nine hundred sixty is the gap versus the minimum price, not cash in hand.

## 7. How the team works — 1:58

**On screen:** **My Farm**, tap **How your team works**. Show Sakha and the four avatars, then the Strands and Cedar sentences.

**Voiceover:** Sakha leads. Each teammate is a tool, which is the Strands Agents pattern, and Cedar keeps them in their lanes. The numbers are Open-Meteo, NOAA, Agmarknet and the PIB support price. A live key can send the words through Amazon Bedrock.

## 8. Close — 2:20

**On screen:** Back to Home. Leave **Act now** and the Ask Sakha button in frame.

**Voiceover:** One dry week. One team. One plan she can act on. Sakha.
